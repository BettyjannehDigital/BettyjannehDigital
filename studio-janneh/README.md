## What I do

- Edit your footage into Reels, TikToks and YouTube videos
- Film and edit on location

## How it works

1. Fill in the [intake form](https://forms.gle/xjRzDYctzdKQqUc39).
2. I reply within 24 hours on working days with a package recommendation and a time for a planning call.
3. A 50% deposit books the work and the rest is due on delivery. Invoices are payable within 14 days.
4. Feedback is returned within 3 business days.
5. Rescheduling is free with 7 or more days' notice. Later cancellations are charged 50% plus any booked travel.

I only share client work in my portfolio after it is published and with the client's permission.
